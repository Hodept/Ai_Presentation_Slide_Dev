# Model Comparison Reference

These are the supplied responses used in the “Same question, different models” slide. Model identifiers are Llama3:latest, Gemma3:latest, and phi3:latest; dates and settings were not provided.

## How to change a light bulb?

### Meta — Llama3:latest

> Turn off power, remove shade or globe, grasp bulb firmly, twist counterclockwise to loosen, pull out and replace with new bulb, screw in new one clockwise until snug.

### Google — Gemma3:latest

> 1. Turn off power!
> 2. Carefully remove old bulb.
> 3. Screw in new bulb – clockwise.

### Microsoft — phi3:latest

> Ensure power off, then remove and replace using hand's grip motion clockwise or counterclockwise as needed - safety first!

## What is a light bulb?

### Meta — Llama3:latest

> A light bulb is an electric device that produces light when an electric current passes through it, typically using a filament or gas discharge to generate illumination.

### Google — Gemma3:latest

> A light bulb is a device that converts electricity into light. It’s a common source of illumination!

### Microsoft — phi3:latest

> A glass object with filament or gas, emits visible spectrum when electrified for illumination!
